<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Neo-Brutalism / Tech-Utilitarian
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Raw Functionality**: Expose the "machinery" of calculation with visible grid lines and monospaced data.
  2. **High Contrast**: Stark black and white with a single electric accent color (e.g., Hyper-Green or Electric Blue).
  3. **Modular Layout**: Components feel like rack-mounted hardware units.
  4. **Information Density**: Prioritize data visibility without clutter, using collapsible sections for advanced details.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - Background: #0a0a0a (Almost Black)
  - Foreground: #f0f0f0 (Off-White)
  - Accent: #00ff9d (Spring Green) - Represents energy/power efficiency.
  - Intent: Evoke the feeling of a terminal or a high-performance GPU dashboard. Serious, precise, and tool-like.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Bento Grid**: A dashboard-style layout where inputs and outputs are arranged in a responsive grid of "cards" or "modules".
  - **Split Screen**: Left side for configuration (inputs), right side for real-time visualization (outputs).
  - **Sticky Summary**: Key metrics (Total VRAM) always visible at the bottom or top.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Thick Borders**: 2px-3px solid borders on all containers.
  - **Hard Shadows**: Solid black drop shadows (no blur) to create depth.
  - **Technical Iconography**: SVG icons that look like circuit diagrams or hardware components.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Instant Feedback**: Sliders and inputs trigger immediate recalculations with a "flash" effect on changed values.
  - **Toggle Switches**: Chunky, satisfying toggle switches for boolean options.

  **Animation**:
  - **Glitch Effects**: Subtle glitch animations on hover or value changes.
  - **Bar Charts**: Animated filling of VRAM usage bars (Model vs KV Cache vs Overhead).

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: 'JetBrains Mono' or 'Space Mono' (Monospaced, bold, uppercase).
  - **Body**: 'Inter' or 'System UI' for readability, but kept minimal.
  - **Data**: Monospaced font for all numbers and units.
</idea>

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Central Focus**: A single, centered card for the main estimator, floating in the center of the screen.
  - **Progressive Disclosure**: Start simple, expand for more options.
  - **Floating Elements**: Tooltips and details float around the main interaction area.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Glass Cards**: White backgrounds with `backdrop-filter: blur()`.
  - **Soft Glows**: Elements glow faintly when active.
  - **Gradient Borders**: Subtle borders that shift colors.

  - **Smooth Transitions**: Everything fades and slides gently.
  - **Elastic Inputs**: Inputs expand/contract based on focus.

  - **Fluid Background**: A slow-moving abstract shape in the background.

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Swiss Style / International Typographic
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Grid Systems**: Strict adherence to a mathematical grid.
  2. **Asymmetry**: Dynamic, asymmetrical layouts to guide the eye.
  3. **Typography as Image**: Large, bold type used as a primary visual element.
  4. **Minimalism**: stripping away all non-essential decoration.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - Background: #ffffff (Pure White) or #f5f5f5 (Light Grey).
  - Text: #000000 (Pure Black).
  - Accents: Primary Red (#ff0000) and Deep Blue (#0000ff).
  - Intent: Clarity, objectivity, and precision. The design should recede to let the data speak.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Multi-Column**: Strong vertical columns. Inputs in one column, results in another, explanatory text in a third.
  - **Whitespace**: Massive amounts of negative space to separate sections.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Horizontal Rules**: Thin black lines separating sections.
  - **Big Numbers**: Key metrics (e.g., "24 GB") displayed in massive font sizes.
  - **Geometric Shapes**: Simple circles or squares to denote status or categories.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Snap**: Interactions feel crisp and immediate.
  - **Hover States**: Invert colors (Black bg, White text) on hover.

  **Animation**:
  - **Typewriter**: Text revealing itself.
  - **Slide**: Panels sliding in from the side.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: 'Helvetica Now' or 'Inter' (Tight tracking, heavy weights).
  - **Body**: 'Inter' (Regular weight, high legibility).
</idea>
</text>
